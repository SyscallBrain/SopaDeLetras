A word search game for Android with no ads, no trackers and no internet access. The only permission is vibration.

<b>Game modes</b>
• Campaign with 300 levels across 10 worlds, progressive unlocking, up to 3 stars per level, timed levels and bosses
• Free play on 6×6, 8×8, 10×10 and 12×12 boards, with three difficulty levels and 10 categories
• Daily challenge: the same puzzle for everyone, generated from the date
• Time attack: find as many words as you can before time runs out
• Mystery word: a clue, words to find and a secret word to guess

<b>Details</b>
• Words in European Portuguese and English
• Hints, statistics, best times and records
• Colorblind mode, adjustable letter size and direction-assisted dragging
• Two dark themes, code-generated sounds and layouts adapted to phones and tablets

The game interface is in Portuguese (pt-PT). Open source under the MIT license.
